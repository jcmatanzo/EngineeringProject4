Size: One Single Dimension Across All Weeks

Decision: we adopt Ø 140 × 75 mm (78 mm maximum in CAD), the dimension already set by the Week 6 Fixed Reflex concept. This way, Week 5 adapts to Week 6 and not the other way around, because the sketch, the CAD specification, and the render were already made with that size.

  • Ø 140 is the PDS maximum (PR-04), so the pod does not grow any further.
  • The pad goes from Ø 90 to Ø 100 mm: +23% striking area (vs. 95 mm) and room for the tension ring and the diaphragm.
  • 75 mm depth: 5 mm more to fit the 17 mm faux leather + EVA pad and the 3D-printing tolerances, without exceeding 80 mm.
  • The floating plate becomes Ø 100 × 3 mm, and the 4 load cells still fit within a Ø ≈ 80 mm circle.

What to change in week5.html so it does not contradict this version: sections C.3, E, and K.1 (they say Ø 130 × 70); C.1 and D.10 (they say Flutter, but the current decision is React Native); the piezo and the HX711 (no longer used); and FR-19 (iOS 14, while React Native requires iOS 15.1 or later). The v2 component list and the v2 communication architecture are already updated with Ø 140 × 75 mm, the faux leather + EVA pad, the 15-LED arc, and the magnetic pogo connector.

FR: Functional Requirements

ID Requirement Verification
FR-01 The system shall light the LED arc stimulus on the pod and at the instant set by the routine, with an inter-pod offset ≤ 10 ms. Photodiode on 2 pods + logic analyzer; 200 stimuli; p95 offset ≤ 10 ms.
FR-02 The system shall measure, inside the pod, the reaction time between LED turn-on and impact, with an error ≤ ±5 ms. Bench with a photodiode on the LED and a reference contact on the striker; ≥ 200 trials; ≥ 90% within ±5 ms.
FR-03 The system shall measure the transmitted force of each punch between 50 and 1,750 N with a peak error ≤ ±15%, flag "saturated" above that range, and give the same result on a wall, floor, or heavy bag. Static: 5–40 kg weights, error ≤ ±3%. Dynamic: known-mass drop (impulse vs. m·Δv), 30 hits per mounting; difference between mountings ≤ 10%.
FR-04 The system shall deliver 100% of a session's events to the app even if the BLE connection is lost for up to 60 s. 100-punch session with 4 pods and 3 forced 60 s dropouts; compare seq on the pods and in SQLite; 0 missing and 0 duplicates.

PR: Performance Requirements

ID Requirement Verification
PR-01 The system shall run ≥ 3 h per charge under the usage profile: 15 LEDs at 40% with a 30% duty cycle, active radio, and ADC at 2 kSPS. Discharge from 4.2 V to cutoff with current logging; 3 units; minimum ≥ 3 h.
PR-02 The pod shall withstand ≥ 10,000 punches of ≈ 1,500 N with no cracks, no loose screws, and a gain drift ≤ 5%, and withstand 3,500 N static load without damage. Pendulum or actuator with inspection every 1,000 hits and recalibration at the end; press with a reference cell at 3,500 N.
PR-03 The system shall distinguish real punches from touches, vibration, drops, and stacking with F1 ≥ 0.90. 300 video-labeled events, split by user; confusion matrix.
PR-04 The pod shall measure Ø ≤ 140 mm and ≤ 78 mm deep, weigh ≤ 0.6 kg, and its stimulus shall be visible at 3 m. Caliper and scale on 5 units; 5 users identify the lit third at 3 m under gym lighting (≥ 9 of 10 correct).

IR: Interface Requirements

ID Requirement Verification
IR-01 The gateway pod shall communicate with the app via BLE 5 GATT (custom service with Control, Events, Ack, Status, and Bulk characteristics), with LE Secure Connections pairing and MTU ≥ 185 B. nRF Connect shows the service and its characteristics; pairing on 1 Android phone and 1 iPhone.
IR-02 The pods shall communicate with each other via ESP-NOW on a fixed channel, up to 8 pods, with SYNC, CFG, STIM, CANCEL, EVT, ACK, STATUS, and ELECT messages, numbered (seq) and HMAC-signed; loss < 1% after ACK. 1 h with 4 pods at 5 m; send, retransmission, and loss counters.
IR-03 The app shall sync with the cloud via HTTPS/REST with JWT, using idempotent insertion by (dev_id, boot_id, seq), and send only aggregated metrics with no personal data to the LLM. Send the same batch twice → 0 duplicates; inspect the payload sent to the LLM.
IR-04 The pod shall charge via USB-C (5 V, 1 A) or via the magnetic pogo connector at the 6 o'clock position, and mount with a quarter-turn locking bayonet (wall, floor, or heavy bag). Full charge ≤ 3 h; 500 mounting cycles; 1,000 punches while wall-mounted without the bayonet rotating.

CR: Constraints

ID Constraint Verification
CR-01 Manufacturing The system shall use a 2-layer PCB with no BGA. Everything hand-soldered shall have a pitch ≥ 0.8 mm; the two fine-pitch ICs (ADS1220 and ADXL375) are assembled only by JLCPCB. FDM enclosure (PETG/ASA) and assembly time ≤ 1.5 h per unit. DRC; list of hand-soldered parts with their pitch; time 3 assemblies.
CR-02 Regulatory The system shall radiate only through the certified ESP32-WROOM-32E module, use a protected 18650 cell, and not be presented as a medical device. It is an academic prototype, not for sale. Document review: module certificates and cell datasheet. Certification for commercial sale remains [Verify].
CR-03 Time & Cost Critical components shall arrive in Mexico within ≤ 2 weeks; 5 units shall be working by Week 13; the prototype shall cost ≤ $3,000 MXN (target ≈ $1,500–1,900) and the 10-unit BOM ≤ $1,400 MXN per pod. Purchase orders with order and arrival dates; invoices.
CR-04 Compatibility The app shall work on Android 8 or later and on iOS 15.1 or later (React Native minimum [Verify with the chosen version]). One full session on ≥ 1 real device per platform.

4. AI Decision

Hybrid: Measure on the Pod, Analyze on the Phone, Write in the Cloud

  1. Measurement runs on the pod (edge), without AI. Reaction time, punch detection, and force need microsecond precision and cannot depend on a network. They are deterministic calculations verified against an instrument (FR-02, FR-03).
  2. Analysis runs on the phone, using rules and statistics. Fatigue (first vs. last third), asymmetry between pods, and trends across sessions work without Internet, and every figure can be explained. A trained model is not justified in the first iteration because there is not enough user data yet.
  3. Only text generation goes to the cloud. A Supabase Edge Function calls the LLM with aggregated metrics (no names, no raw series), and the API key lives on the server. A verifier checks that every figure in the generated text exists in the input JSON. If the service fails, the app falls back to templates. This keeps cost, latency (2–10 s, asynchronous), and privacy risk bounded.

8. Weekly Questions

Question Answer
Where does the AI run, and why? In two places: rules and statistics on the phone (works without Internet and explains every figure), and LLM text generation in the cloud (aggregated metrics only, with a template fallback). The pod does not run AI: it measures with deterministic calculations because it needs microsecond precision and verification against an instrument.
Which protocol, and why? BLE 5 GATT between the app and a single gateway pod, because it is native on Android and iOS, doesn't change the phone's network, and the phone doesn't have to maintain N connections. ESP-NOW between pods, because it provides millisecond latency without a router and lets the gateway synchronize the stimuli. HTTPS to the cloud, with deferred sync. Reaction time does not depend on any of them: it is measured inside the pod.
Hardest component to source, and plan B? The ADXL375: single manufacturer, no confirmed stock in Mexican stores, 64 pieces on LCSC, and a price of USD 22.73 at 10 units. Plan B: an H3LIS331DL on a module; if that doesn't arrive either, force is still measured by the load cells and the accelerometer stays in only 1–2 units.
What is the difference between the Week 13 deliverable and an exploration prototype? An exploration prototype is one unit built with modules and a breadboard to answer a technical question (do the load cells capture the peak?). The Week 13 deliverable is a first product iteration: 5 identical units with our custom PCB, frozen and versioned firmware, per-unit calibration and end-of-line testing, a BOM backed by invoices, and use by a third party without the team present, verified against the 16 requirements in this PDS.

9. Defense of a Design Decision

Measuring Force with 4 Load Cells Under a Floating Plate, Not Just the Accelerometer

"If the pod is mounted on a wall, floor, or heavy bag, the acceleration changes with the mounting; the force between the plate and the chassis does not."
  1. Problem: with only an accelerometer, F = m·a depends on the effective mass and on whatever is behind the pod. The same punch gives different numbers on a wall and on the floor (contradiction C2 from Week 5), and Fixed Reflex mounts in three different ways.
  2. Decision: 4 × 50 kg load cells under a Ø 100 × 3 mm aluminum plate, read by an ADS1220 at 2 kSPS (≈ 18–28 samples during the 9–14 ms rise of a punch), with hard stops at 90% load and a saturation flag. The ADXL375 is kept for impact detection and for the range above 1,750 N.
  3. Cost and risk: ≈ $215 MXN per pod at 10 units and a medium schedule risk. It is mitigated with a load cell test bench in Week 2 and a go/no-go decision at the end of Week 3; the fallback is the ADXL375 alone with the calibrated index.
  4. How it is proven: FR-03. Weights of 5–40 kg (±3%), a known-mass drop (±15% at the peak), and the same series on wall, floor, and heavy bag (difference ≤ 10%).

11. Reflection and Key Takeaways

Reviewing our first version against the rubric made us realize that a PDS is not a long document holding everything we researched; it is a single page that someone outside the team can pick up and use to check whether the product actually meets its goals. Rewriting every requirement as "The system shall…", with a concrete figure and a verification method, forced us to commit to values we had previously left open as hypotheses, and it exposed how many of our original criteria could not really be tested.

The second lesson was that cost is a moving target. The accelerometer's price more than tripled in a single week, which pushed our BOM from $815 to $1,390 MXN and our reference price above $4,600 MXN. From now on, critical components are verified on the day of purchase, ordered early from more than one supplier, and always paired with a plan B. Aligning Week 5 with the Week 6 dimensions also showed us that keeping one consistent source of truth across deliverables saves far more time than it costs.
